# Davi Seemann

> **Financial structuring for complex projects — and the data & ML behind the decisions.**

I grew up reading balance sheets before I learned calculus. Engineer by training, finance by origin.

I do financial modeling, resource management and capital allocation for clients, and I build the data systems and ML behind those decisions. At **BRDE**, a regional development bank, I work on structuring municipal PPPs — and that's the direction I'm building toward: **project finance and the funding of complex projects**, with engineering and models as the edge.

In research at **GAMA/UFSC**, I study uncertainty estimation in deep learning: a model that knows when it doesn't know beats a confident wrong one.
